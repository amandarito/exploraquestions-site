Support

Something not working?

Email us and a person will answer — usually within two business days.

support@exploraquestions.app

Please include your device and app version (Profile → About) so we can help faster.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Profile → Upgrade to Premium and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so cancel there: iPhone — Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ExploraQuestions. Android — Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Where are my reflections stored, and can I get them out?

On your device. To take a copy with you, go to Profile → Export journal and choose plain text or PDF.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Delete all reflections erases everything from the device immediately and can't be undone. Deleting the app also removes it. If you'd like written confirmation, email privacy@exploraquestions.app.

I missed a day. Did I lose my streak?

Yes, the streak resets — but nothing else does. Every question you've already answered stays discovered, and yesterday's question is still there to write on.

Can I turn the daily reminder off?

Profile → Daily Reminder. Change the time or switch it off entirely.

If you opt in, the app sends anonymous crash reports so we can fix bugs. These contain no journal content. You can turn this off in Profile → About.
